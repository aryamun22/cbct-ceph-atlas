Recomputed every frame from the landmarks that ride on each piece, with the definitions of the project's movement simulator (sim.py): lateral-ceph projection in the Frankfort frame, bilateral points averaged. Chips: green within 1 SD of the male norm, amber within 2 SD, red beyond. Hard tissue only: soft tissue (lips, nose, chin pad) is out of scope here.

Each preset animates the pieces with the simulator's rules (the mandible is propped or seated on the occlusion). Presets add to the current plan; hand moves made since the last plan are replaced (Undo brings them back).
